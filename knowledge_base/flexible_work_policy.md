# Flexible Work Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to two days per week with prior
manager approval. Team members must remain available during core collaboration
hours from 09:00 to 15:00 South African Standard Time.

Remote work is not an automatic entitlement. A manager may require office
attendance for client meetings, training, regulatory activities, or other
business needs. Company information must be accessed only through approved
devices and the corporate VPN.
