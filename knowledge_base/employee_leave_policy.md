# Employee Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Permanent employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar
year. Leave must be requested in the HR portal and approved by the employee's
line manager before travel is booked. Up to five unused days may be carried
into the next calendar year and must be used by 31 March.

## Maternity and parental leave

Employees who meet the eligibility requirements receive six months of paid
maternity leave. Eligible partners receive ten working days of paid parental
leave. Employees should notify HR at least four weeks before the planned leave
start date when practicable.

## Sick leave

An employee who is absent for more than two consecutive working days must
provide a valid medical certificate. The employee must notify the line manager
as soon as reasonably possible on the first day of absence.
